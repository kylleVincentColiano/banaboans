A city with deep roots

A green gateway of Davao Del Norte

Panabo lies in Davao del Norte in the Davao Region of Mindanao, positioned along the busy Davao–Tagum corridor. Long associated with agricultural settlement and trade, it became a component city in 2001.

Its name is commonly linked to the historic "pana-sa-baboy," and the city has grown into an important agro-industrial center. Banana production remains its signature while agriculture, processing, trade, and manufacturing continue to broaden the city's future.

Population 211,242 residents (2024 census)
Languages Cebuano, Filipino & English
Key industries Banana, agri-processing & logistics
Setting Northern Davao Gulf area

Landmarks & local experiences

See the many sides of Panabo

Find quiet green spaces, public landmarks, and easy day-trip escapes around the city and nearby communities.

01

Tadeco Road

TADECO Banana Plantation is one of the major attractions associated with Panabo City, the “Banana Capital of the Philippines.” TADECO was founded in 1950 and became one of the country’s major producers of Cavendish bananas.

Photo stop

02

Panbo Seawall

Panabo Seawall can be considered one of the most beautiful places because it combines the relaxing view of the Davao Gulf, fresh sea breeze, peaceful atmosphere, and a beautiful place to watch the sunset. It is also a popular local spot for walking, relaxing, and spending time with family or friends.

Mangrove Eco Park & Boardwalk

Panabo's mangrove area, including the J.P. Laurel boardwalk, connects visitors to the city's coastal ecology. It's a reminder of how the wetlands shelter shorelines, and a favorite for slow walks and photos.

BEFORE PANABO BECAME A CITY

A community rooted in its indigenous past

Long before Panabo became a city, its fertile lowlands were home to indigenous communities who lived closely with the land. Among the earliest inhabitants were Aeta communities, whose way of life was shaped by hunting, gathering, and the natural environment.

The first inhabitants

The Aeta people lived in the area long before modern Panabo emerged. Their traditional use of the bow and arrow for hunting is also connected to the origin of the name Panabo, from pana-sa-boboy.

"Before the city, there was a community shaped by the land."

ECONOMY AND LIFE OF PANABO

ang pamumuhay sa cagangohan

Life in Cagangohan was deeply connected to the sea. Fishing was an important part of daily life, providing food and livelihood for families. The waters along the coast supported a simple way of living built around fishing, shared work, and strong community ties.

2025–2026 news & updates

Panabo’s economy is becoming more connected and industrial

Recent developments highlight Panabo’s continuing role in agriculture, export manufacturing, logistics, and regional investment.

Economy September 2026

Anflo Industrial Estate reaches 24 operational locators

Anflo Industrial Estate in Panabo now hosts 24 operational locators, with about 70% engaged in export-oriented manufacturing. The estate’s activities include agro-processing, packaging, warehousing, cold-chain services, and other manufacturing operations.

Infrastructure August 2026

New power substation strengthens Anflo Industrial Estate

Davao Light inaugurated the San Pedro Substation at Anflo Industrial Estate to support the estate’s growing power requirements. The first phase provides 10 MVA of capacity, with an additional 33 MVA planned for Phase 2, targeted for completion by December 2027.

Economy September 2026

Panabo attracts export-oriented manufacturing

Locators at Anflo Industrial Estate include HEAD Sport, Novococonut, and GMAC Logitech Refrigeration. Their presence is expanding Panabo’s role in sporting-goods production, coconut-based food products, refrigeration, logistics, and cold-chain services.

Infrastructure August 2026

Agro-industrial estate continues to expand

The 63-hectare PEZA-accredited Anflo Industrial Estate reported occupancy of approximately 85% to 89% and nearly 2,000 workers in August 2026. Its location near the Davao City International Container Terminal supports Panabo’s position in regional supply chains.

Community August 2026

LAMDAG government information exhibit opens

The four-day LAMDAG Government Information Exhibit 2026 opened on August 26 as part of the city’s public-information and civic-engagement activities, bringing government agencies and services closer to Panabo residents.

Community 2025–2026

Panabo’s population reaches 211,242

The 2024 Census of Population recorded 211,242 residents in Panabo. The city’s continued growth increases the need for coordinated transport, utilities, housing, public services, and infrastructure planning across its 40 barangays.
